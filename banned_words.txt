CATEGORY 7: COMMENT SLOP OPENERS AND CLOSERS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════

These identify a comment as machine-written
regardless of how good the middle is. The shape
(opens with praise, closes with a question) is
the tell even when every word in between is
fine. Source: 90 days of this account's
published_comments archive + the 10-24 Jada
Balster signal (CMO post complaining about AI
comments that are "longer than the original
post but say absolutely nothing").

BANNED AS OPENERS (any comment, any mode):
❌ "Great post" / "Great insight" / "Amazing
   post" / "Such a great reminder"
❌ "So true" / "So true this"
❌ "Love this" / "Love where this is going"
❌ "This resonates" / "This hits home"
❌ "Couldn't agree more" / "100%" / "Absolutely"
❌ "Well said" / "Kudos to" / "Hats off to"
❌ "This is why I follow you"
❌ "Here's the thing" (banned as a comment
   opener only; allowed mid-post, max once per
   post)
❌ "Here's what I learned" / "Something I've
   learned" (these open a post, not a comment)
❌ "I think you're right that" (flattery setup)

BANNED AS CLOSERS (any comment, any mode):
❌ "Thanks for sharing" / "Thanks for putting
   this out there"
❌ "Looking forward to" (anything)
❌ "Happy to dive deeper" / "Happy to discuss"
❌ "Would love to hear more about" (generic)
❌ Any restatement of the post's own point
❌ A question mark, except in INTERACTIVE reply
   mode, and then only when the thread needs
   the next move
❌ Hashtags inside a comment

WHY: a comment that opens with a compliment and
closes with a question is the shape of a
template. The argument test (voice_dna.txt
PART 10) is the fix: at least one line the
author could argue with, and a flat ending.

POSITIVE ALTERNATIVES SUMMARY
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════

INSTEAD OF EMPTY WORDS, USE:

For emphasis:
✅ Specific numbers ("40% improvement" vs "significant")
✅ Concrete examples ("Here's what happened...")
✅ Real names/tools ("I used X" vs "we leverage")

For transitions (posts only; banned as comment
openers, see CATEGORY 7):
✅ "And" / "But" / "So"
✅ "Here's the thing" (mid-post, max once)
✅ "Here's what I mean"

For enthusiasm:
✅ Specific praise with reason
✅ Describing actual impact
✅ Personal story showing the value

For expertise:
✅ Share what actually worked
✅ Include what didn't work
✅ Show your reasoning
✅ Admit uncertainty when honest

THE ULTIMATE TEST
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════

Before publishing, read aloud:

"Would I actually say these words to a friend 
at a coffee shop?"

If NO → Rewrite in your natural voice.
If YES → Publish with confidence.

Real people don't say "leverage synergies to unlock 
cutting-edge value propositions."

Real people say "let's use what we have to build 
something new."

Write like you talk.
Just cleaner.
