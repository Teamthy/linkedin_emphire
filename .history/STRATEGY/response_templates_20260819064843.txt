╔═══════════════════════════════════════════════╗
║ SECTION 1: COMMENT TEMPLATES                   ║
║ (For commenting on others' posts)              ║
╚═══════════════════════════════════════════════╝

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════
COMMENT TYPE 1: ADDING EXPERTISE
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════

Template:
"[SPECIFIC REFERENCE to their point]. In my experience 
working on [RELEVANT PROJECT], I've found that 
[YOUR ADDITIONAL INSIGHT]. The part that surprised me 
was [UNEXPECTED DETAIL]. Have you seen [RELATED 
QUESTION]?"

Example:
"The point about database indexing is spot on. In my 
work building e-commerce systems on Shopify, I found 
that composite indexes changed everything for query 
performance. What surprised me was how much it 
affected write speeds too. Have you tried partial 
indexes for this use case?"

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════
COMMENT TYPE 2: PERSONAL STORY CONNECTION
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════

Template:
"This hits home. [BRIEF PERSONAL STORY — 2 sentences]. 
The lesson I took from it was [YOUR TAKEAWAY]. 
Thanks for putting this into words."

Example:
"This hits home. When I transitioned from medical lab 
science to software engineering, the biggest challenge 
wasn't the code — it was unlearning my old identity. 
The lesson I took was that consistency beats credentials 
every time. Thanks for putting this into words."

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════
COMMENT TYPE 3: RESPECTFUL CHALLENGE
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════

Template:
"Interesting perspective. I've seen this from a 
slightly different angle though — [YOUR DIFFERENT 
VIEW in 2-3 sentences]. That said, [ACKNOWLEDGE 
THEIR VALID POINT]. Curious what you think about 
[SPECIFIC QUESTION]?"

Example:
"Interesting perspective. I've seen this differently 
though — in my work adding AI to existing software, 
I've found that most apps genuinely don't need AI. 
The simpler solution usually wins. That said, your 
point about user expectations is valid. Curious — 
how do you decide when AI adds real value versus 
when it's just added complexity?"

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════
COMMENT TYPE 4: QUESTION THAT SHOWS DEPTH
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════

Template:
"[ACKNOWLEDGE their insight]. Something I've been 
thinking about: [THOUGHTFUL QUESTION that shows 
you understand the complexity]. I'm asking because 
[BRIEF REASON from your experience]."

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════
COMMENT TYPE 5: BRIEF AGREEMENT WITH VALUE ADD
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════

Template:
"[SPECIFIC POINT] is exactly right. I'd add one 
thing: [YOUR ADDITIONAL VALUE — 1-2 sentences]. 
This changed how I approach [RELATED TOPIC]."

╔═══════════════════════════════════════════════╗
║ SECTION 2: REPLY TEMPLATES                     ║
║ (For replying to comments on YOUR posts)       ║
╚═══════════════════════════════════════════════╝

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════
REPLY TO: AGREEMENT / PRAISE
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════

Template:
"[NAME] — [ACKNOWLEDGE SPECIFIC POINT they made]. 
[ADD VALUE or ASK FOLLOW-UP]. What's your experience 
with [RELATED TOPIC]?"

DO NOT USE:
- "Thanks for sharing!"
- "Appreciate the kind words!"
- "Glad this resonated!"

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════
REPLY TO: DISAGREEMENT / CHALLENGE
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════

Template:
"[NAME] — honest take, and I appreciate you pushing 
back. [ACKNOWLEDGE THEIR POINT — 1 sentence]. 
Where I see it differently: [YOUR REASONING — 
1-2 sentences]. Probably depends on [VARIABLE]. 
What's been your specific experience?"

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════
REPLY TO: QUESTION
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════

Template:
"[NAME] — great question. [DIRECT ANSWER — 
2-3 sentences]. In practice, I've found [SPECIFIC 
EXAMPLE]. Want me to go deeper on this in a future 
post?"

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════
REPLY TO: PERSONAL STORY SHARED
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════

Template:
"[NAME] — really appreciate you sharing that. 
[REFERENCE SPECIFIC DETAIL from their story]. 
The fact that you [WHAT THEY DID] shows [QUALITY]. 
That takes [COURAGE/DISCIPLINE/ETC]."

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════
REPLY TO: SHORT/GENERIC COMMENT
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════

Template:
"[NAME] — thanks! Curious though — what part stood 
out to you most? Always learning what hits hardest 
for people."

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════

learning more, but I want to make sure it's a 
genuine fit before either of us invests time.

Could you share:
- The JD or more details about the role?
- The compensation range?
- Team structure?

That would help me give you a more informed response."
