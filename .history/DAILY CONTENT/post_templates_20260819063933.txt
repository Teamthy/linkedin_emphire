TEMPLATE USAGE GUIDE
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════

WEEKLY ROTATION EXAMPLE:

Monday: Template 1 (Technical) OR Template 7 (Framework)
Tuesday: Template 2 (Story) OR Template 6 (Observation)
Wednesday: Template 9 (Opinion) OR Template 3 (Coaching)
Thursday: Template 4 (Leadership) OR Template 5 (Faith)
Friday: Template 14 (Conversation) OR Template 11 (BTS)

RULES:
- Never use same template two days in row
- Mix technical + personal + wisdom weekly
- Always fill [BRACKETS] with specific details
- Always run voice check before publishing
- Always include question at end for engagement
