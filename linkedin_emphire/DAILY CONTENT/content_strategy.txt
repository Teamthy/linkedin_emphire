╔═══════════════════════════════════════════════╗
║          PART 2: CONTENT STYLES                ║
║           (HOW I TELL THE STORY)                ║
╚═══════════════════════════════════════════════╝

I use 14 different content styles. Never default to
just one. Rotate through these for variety and depth.

STYLE 1: TECHNICAL DEEP-DIVES
What: Detailed technical content with code, patterns,
architecture discussions.
When: Establishing engineering credibility
Example: "Here's how I structured our authentication
system to handle 10,000 concurrent users..."

STYLE 2: PERSONAL STORIES
What: Real moments from my life, career transitions,
failures, wins.
When: Building emotional connection
Example: "3 years ago I was running blood tests.
Today I built an AI system. Here's the story..."

STYLE 3: LIFE COACHING
What: Discipline, mindset, focus, purpose, direction.
When: Inspiring and guiding
Example: "The 3 disciplines that separate engineers
who grow from those who plateau..."

STYLE 4: LEADERSHIP LESSONS
What: Team building, decisions, influence, vision.
When: Speaking to senior engineers and leaders
Example: "The leadership mistake I made that cost
me 2 team members..."

STYLE 5: FAITH-INSPIRED WISDOM
What: Character, integrity, purpose — professionally
framed.
When: Sundays or reflective moments; use sparingly
Example: "The Bible principle that transformed how
I code..."

STYLE 6: OBSERVATIONAL INSIGHTS
What: Things I notice in Lagos, tech, life, turned
into meaningful reflections.
When: Fresh observations from daily life
Example: "I noticed something at a Lagos market
yesterday that applies to software engineering..."

STYLE 7: EDUCATIONAL FRAMEWORKS
What: Step-by-step guides, mental models, systems
others can copy.
When: Teaching a specific concept
Example: "The 5-step framework I use to evaluate
any new technology..."

STYLE 8: BOOK LESSONS
What: Insights from what I'm reading, connected to
real work.
When: Genuinely learned from a book
Example: "I just finished [book]. Here are 3 lessons
that apply to software engineering..."

STYLE 9: OPINION PIECES
What: Bold takes on tech, AI hype, careers,
education, Africa.
When: Strong, defensible position
Example: "Unpopular opinion: The AI engineer role
will disappear in 3 years. Here's why..."

STYLE 10: FICTIONAL STORIES/PARABLES
What: Made-up scenarios that illustrate real
principles.
When: A real story doesn't exist but the lesson
is important
Framing: "Imagine this...", "Here's a story...",
"Sometimes I think about a hypothetical..."
MUST be clearly framed as fictional.

STYLE 11: BEHIND-THE-SCENES
What: Building in public, project updates, real work.
When: Interesting current work
Example: "Here's what I'm building this week..."

STYLE 12: INSPIRATIONAL NARRATIVES
What: Real or observed stories of transformation.
When: Genuinely moving story
Example: "I met an engineer last week whose story
changed how I think about persistence..."

STYLE 13: VOLUNTEERING & COMMUNITY
What: Lessons from serving, church, community
building.
When: Real community involvement to share
Example: "Volunteering at [event] taught me something
about team dynamics..."

STYLE 14: CONVERSATION-STARTERS
What: Questions, hot takes designed to spark dialogue.
When: Maximum comments
Example: "Software engineers: What's the one thing
you wish you knew when you started?"

╔═══════════════════════════════════════════════╗
║         PART 3: CONTENT STRATEGY RULES         ║
╚═══════════════════════════════════════════════╝

RULE 1: WEEKLY BALANCE
In any given week, mix pillars:
- 2 technical (Software Engineering focus)
- 1 AI/CV/Ethics specialization
- 1 leadership/career
- 1 personal/life

RULE 2: STYLE ROTATION
Don't use the same style twice in a row.

RULE 3: PILLAR ROTATION
Don't post from same pillar two days running.

RULE 4: SOFTWARE ENGINEERING FIRST
Always position Software Engineering as your
identity. AI, CV, e-commerce, DeFi are your
specializations WITHIN software engineering.

RULE 5: CULTURAL AUTHENTICITY
Weave Nigerian/African perspective naturally,
not forced.

RULE 6: ANTI-HYPE POSITIONING
Consistently choose depth over trends.
Real work over popular topics.

RULE 7: TEACHING MENTALITY
Frame content as helping others, not showcasing
yourself.

RULE 8: MULTI-DOMAIN SHOWCASE
Regularly reference your diverse work (not just AI).

RULE 9: ETHICAL RESPONSIBILITY
Include AI ethics perspectives regularly.
1-2 ethics posts per week.

RULE 10: FICTION CLARITY
Always clearly frame fictional content as fiction.
Never present parables as personal experience.

PART 4: DATA UPDATE (2026-10-23) — WHAT THE
ANALYTICS SAY, AND HOW THE STRATEGY BENDS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════

Source: LINKEDIN/performance_tracker.md, snapshot
2026-10-23 (90-day window, 684 followers).
Parts 1-3 are the menu. This part is the evidence of
what the audience actually ate. Where they conflict,
this part wins until the next 90-day refresh.

WHAT WORKS (verified on 90-day data)
1. Comments on other people's posts. Best comment
   (Paolo Perrone thread) = 60,500 impressions vs
   best feed post 1,053. Comments ride into the
   author's audience; feed posts are capped by the
   follower base.
2. Proof-of-work specificity. "4 of 6 attempts
   worked", "three characters changed", "0.13 point
   margin". Numbers a reader can re-run beat
   adjectives.
3. The check frame (claim vs check, re-run, written
   pass) applied as a lens to other people's topics.
   All of the 90-day top 3 sit in this family.
4. Group posts. The 90-day top performer is a post
   in group 6773411. Free distribution plus a
   group-specific question close.
5. Teaser then publish cadence. The 10-12 teaser
   (753 imp) set up article 2. The pre-show works.
6. Question closes on posts. 26 of 103 engagements
   are comments (~25%). This audience argues. Feed
   it.

WHAT DOES NOT WORK (verified)
1. Introspective micro. Lowest performers in the
   archive (138 imp class). Not worth the slot.
2. Saves and reposts: 0 in 90 days. Content is
   read and argued, not shared or bookmarked. The
   next format to test is the re-runnable test list
   (the 30-minute test, the 15-minute test) as a
   standalone, to earn the first save.
3. Search: 2 appearances per week. Not a channel at
   this size. Do not spend words on SEO phrasing.
4. Profile conversion: 285 views in 90 days against
   3,838 reached (~7%). The profile is the weakest
   link in the funnel after a comment lands.

WHAT THE DATA CHANGES
1. Channel hierarchy: (1) comments on tier 1-2
   surfaces, (2) group posts, (3) feed posts. Feed
   posts are now for depth and for the profile
   funnel, not for reach.
2. Pacing: 5-8 comment actions per day, spaced,
   never a burst. Follow-up replies are actions.
3. Positioning: AI red teaming / check frame is the
   engine (VERIFIED_POSTS rows 6-15 are all
   AREDTEAM). The parts 1-3 pillars stay as the
   secondary menu, run through the check lens when
   they pass it.
4. KPIs to watch weekly: comment imp vs post imp,
   profile view rate, out-of-network share (66%
   baseline), saves, follower pace (1,000 Premium
   threshold expected mid-to-late November; Premium
   stays off until it clears).
5. Surface discipline: daily picks come from
   ENGAGEMENT/comment_surfaces.md, not the raw
   feed. Demote and log surfaces that stop paying.
