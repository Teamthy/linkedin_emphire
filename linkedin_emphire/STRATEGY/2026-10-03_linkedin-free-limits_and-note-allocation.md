## WHAT WE JUST FOUND OUT (the hard way)

1. **No InMail on free accounts.** You cannot DM anyone you are not connected to. The "message" button on a 3rd+ profile shows a Premium upsell, nothing more. InMail is Premium only.
2. **Personalized connection notes are quota'd on free accounts.** LinkedIn does not publish the number. Reports from Oct 2025 through Aug 2026 range from 5 to 20 per month, most commonly cited around 5. After the quota, you can still send requests, but blank ones.
3. Free note cap is 200 characters. Premium raises it to 300 and removes the monthly quota within the normal weekly connection caps.

## WHAT THIS DOES TO THE PLAN

The 5-notes-per-week outreach schedule in the playbook assumes unlimited notes. On a free account it is not achievable. October has roughly 5 quota'd notes if the low estimate is right.
