# Breaking AI series, Article 2 visual design

Built 2026-10-12 (night) for the 2026-10-13 10:00 WAT publish. All assets in this
directory are generated by `/home/user/art2_visuals/build.py` (PIL), so any copy
change is a re-render, not a redraw. PNGs stay untracked, same as card1.

## The system (matches the existing card1)

- Background: `#0C1A2C` dark navy with a faint vertical grid line every ~54 px (`#13253D`).
- Accent: `#8FB6E0` light blue. One accent word or number per asset, always.
- Text: white `#FFFFFF` for headline and body, muted `#8296AC` for supporting lines.
- Type: DejaVu Sans Bold for headlines and numbers, DejaVu Sans Regular for body.
- Header (every asset): left `AI RED TEAM LOG · EP 002` in accent, right `BREAKING AI / 02 OF 21` in muted, letter-spaced.
- Footer (every asset): light blue bar `#A6C1E1`, dark text `OLUSANYA TIMOTHY || SOFTWARE ENGINEER · AI RED TEAMING`, letter-spaced. The `||` is the series separator (never an em dash).
- Margins: 64 px all sides. Panels `#12233B` with a 4 px accent left rule.

## Copy rules baked into the assets

- No em dashes anywhere in the copy. Separators are commas, periods, and the `||` footer mark.
- No faces on any asset (the AI face candidates were rejected; no crop is in the sandbox).
- Numbers in words for the Pidgin-stat line ("one time in forty") to match the article body.
- Every card is a claim the article can defend: the five mode names, the checklist, and the 1-in-40 stat are all verbatim or near-verbatim from `article.md`.

## If a change is needed after publish

Edit the string in `build.py`, run `python3 /home/user/art2_visuals/build.py`, and
re-upload the one affected file. Sizes and layouts are pinned per asset, so a
one-line copy change will not break the composition unless it roughly doubles
in length.
