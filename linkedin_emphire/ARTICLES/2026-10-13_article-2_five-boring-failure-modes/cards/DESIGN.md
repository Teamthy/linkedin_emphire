# Breaking AI series, Article 2 visual design

Built 2026-10-12 (night) for the 2026-10-13 10:00 WAT publish, rebuilt 2026-10-13
to the article 1 standard. Every asset in this directory is generated by the
series engine `linkedin_emphire/DESIGN/breaking_ai_engine.py` (PIL, config
driven), so any copy change is a re-render, not a redraw. PNGs stay untracked,
same as card1.

## The two systems

### Light system (the article 1 standard)

Article 1 published with three light/photo templates. The engine re-creates
them for every article from `ARTICLE_N` config values:

- Save card `1080x1350`: near-white `#F4F6F9`, grey-blue header labels
  (`#8494A6` left, `#8FB6D9` right, letter-spaced), huge black uppercase
  headline `#14181D`, grey subtitle `#5C6B7A` with a short light-blue underline
  `#9DBFE0`, numbered rows (numerals `#A9C7E4`, text `#1A2530`, dividers
  `#E3E9EF`), dark navy footer bar `#0F1722` with white CTA left and
  `#BreakingAI` in `#4D9FDB` right.
- Hero portrait `1080x1350`: dark scene photo (cover-cropped, left
  legibility gradient), cyan banner top-left `#03A9D6` with black letter-spaced
  text and two diagonal slashes, white headline with one line boxed in cyan
  (black text on the box), white sub line plus cyan sub line.
- Hero landscape `1600x900`: same treatment, plus a white name tag
  (black text, "Olusanya Timothy") top-left and `BREAKING AI · #NN/21` white
  letter-spaced top-right, sub lines with a thin white left rule.
- Scene photos are generated per article (no real faces) and cover-cropped to
  size by the engine, so the exact photo dimensions do not matter.

### Dark system (the card1 teaser system)

- Background `#0C1A2C` dark navy with a faint vertical grid line every ~54 px
  (`#13253D`). Accent `#8FB6E0` light blue, one accent word or number per
  asset, always.
- Text: white for headline and body, muted `#8296AC` for supporting lines.
- Type: DejaVu Sans Bold for headlines and numbers, DejaVu Sans Regular for
  body (the only bold font in the sandbox; close enough to the reference cut).
- Header (every asset): left `AI RED TEAM LOG · EP 002` in accent, right
  `BREAKING AI / 02 OF 21` in muted, letter-spaced.
- Footer (every asset): light blue bar `#A6C1E1`, dark text
  `OLUSANYA TIMOTHY || SOFTWARE ENGINEER · AI RED TEAMING`, letter-spaced.
  The `||` is the series separator (never an em dash).
- Margins 64 px all sides. Panels `#12233B` with a 4 px accent left rule.

## Copy rules baked into the assets

- No em dashes anywhere in the copy. Separators are commas, periods, and the
  `||` footer mark. (The article 1 reference images themselves contain a
  baked-in em dash and a misspelled "Olushanya"; new assets do not copy
  either of those.)
- No faces on any asset. Scene photos show two unnamed engineers in a dark
  office, matching the article 1 hero scenes.
- Numbers in words for the Pidgin-stat line ("one time in forty") to match the
  article body.
- Every card is a claim the article can defend: the five mode names, the
  checklist, and the 1-in-40 stat are all verbatim or near-verbatim from
  `article.md`.

## Reusing the engine for articles 3 to 21

```python
from breaking_ai_engine import build_article, ARTICLE_2
import copy

cfg = copy.deepcopy(ARTICLE_2)
cfg["num"] = 3
cfg["cards_dir"] = "<article dir>/cards"
cfg["save_card"]["title_lines"] = [...]
cfg["save_card"]["rows"] = [...]
# ... edit the other copy blocks, set the two hero photo paths ...
build_article(cfg)
```

Headline sizes auto-fit to the canvas, the cyan banner sizes to its text, and
wrapping is word-aware, so a normal copy swap will not break the composition.
Generate the two scene photos first (one portrait, one landscape, dark office,
empty dark space on the left third) and point the config at them.

## If a change is needed after publish

Edit the `ARTICLE_2` config in `breaking_ai_engine.py`, run
`python3 linkedin_emphire/DESIGN/breaking_ai_engine.py`, and re-upload the one
affected file. Layouts are pinned per asset.
