Mentorship isn't asked for. It's offered when someone sees consistency, curiosity, and seriousness. 6–8 week playbook:

### Week 3 — Value add (no ask)
Use the VALUE-ADD FOLLOW UP template:
```

MCP security] and thought of our chat. Reminded me of your structural-integrity
framing.

No response needed — just thought you'd find it interesting.
```

### Week 4–6 — Build in public in his direction
- Keep commenting on his stuff.
- Post publicly about your own AI security learning journey — that's the #1 thing senior people notice. Someone learning in public stands out.

  ```

  if you have 30 seconds — no pressure either way.
  ```

### Week 6–8 — The call ask (still not "be my mentor")
Use the DEEPER CONNECTION template once the relationship is warm (he's replied to comments/DMs at least 3 times, or you've had real back-and-forth):

- Follow through on anything you said you'd share
- After 2–3 calls over 2–3 months where you're clearly learning, applying, and reporting back — THEN ask formally:
  ```

  ```
