## COMMENT (paste this)
Specific numbers, which is rare in these posts. Most skip straight to the philosophy.

The part worth flagging: those Slack follow-ups and review pings now carry your agency's voice to your clients. An internal ping can be wrong and everyone shrugs. An outbound follow-up sent to the wrong person, or fired on stale context, reads as you being careless, and the client never knows a system sent it.

So the line I would draw: agents can be free inside the building and gated outside it. Anything that reaches a client is either drafted for a human to send, or capped to things that cannot embarrass you.
