## COMMENT (paste this)
The grounded theory framing is doing real work here, and it explains why the method survives contact with AI when dashboards don't.

One gap worth naming though: grounded theory depends on what the observer can actually observe. A designer watching the interface sees the interaction, the tone, the moment it feels wrong. They cannot see that retrieval pulled the wrong chunk three steps back, or that the tool call inherited a service account with more scope than the task needed. The failures that cost the most money are invisible from the UI.

So the field notes need two observers or one observer with access to the layer underneath: what the model retrieved, what it called, what it was allowed to call.

The part that holds up regardless: notes become tests, and tests rerun after every change. That loop is the whole thing.
