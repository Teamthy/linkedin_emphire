I keep seeing this take pop up — "AI is a hoax" or "AI will end humanity."

Here's the thing: both sides are avoiding the real conversation.

As a software engineer in Lagos building AI integrations, I can tell you the scary stuff isn't Skynet. It's the boring failures that happen in production when teams ship fast and test later.

PII leaks. Silent bias against non-Western names. Deepfakes in elections where fact-checking barely exists. Medical advice from a chatbot for someone who can't afford a second opinion.

The truth is we already know how to handle powerful technology. We did it with airplanes, medicine, cars, nuclear energy. You build guardrails before the crashes force you to.

One rule I'm building by: if the cost of your AI failing falls on people who aren't in the room making the decision, you're not building fast — you're building recklessly.

PS — Am I the only one tired of the AI debate being only two extreme options? 👇
