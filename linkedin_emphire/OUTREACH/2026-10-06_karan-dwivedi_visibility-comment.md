## COMMENT (paste this)
Fourth practice, and it compounds harder than the other three: publish some of it.

A memo makes your judgment auditable inside one company. Publishing the same reasoning makes it auditable outside, with your name on it. That is the difference between being known internally and being findable from outside.

The security version matters more now. As more code gets written with AI, the reasoning behind a control decision is the part a human still owns. "We chose this mitigation, here is what we accepted" survives a model change, a re-org, and an incident review.

I write a public series on breaking AI systems for exactly this reason. Same muscle as the memo, wider room.
