The three shifts you laid out are right. I'd add one KPI I think teams are going to start measuring seriously soon: time from merge to first production incident on that code.

When an AI writes 100 lines in a prompt and a human skims and approves it, the code looks clean, the PR ships fast, and the author-velocity metric goes up. But the bug rate, revert rate, and weekend pager volume tell the real story six weeks later.

Teams that win in the AI-coding era won't measure how fast their engineers prompt. They'll measure how little their shipped code breaks. Same as it ever was — the boring metrics just stopped being optional.
