## REPLY (paste this)
The screen doing the objecting is the right instinct. The catch is habituation. Provenance labels become wallpaper within about two weeks, and then people scroll past them the same way they scroll past a cookie banner.

So the label has to change behavior, not just sit there. No source or no date means the agent should be blocked from asserting it, or forced to say it is unverified out loud. Missing provenance is the dangerous case, and most implementations quietly treat "no date found" as probably fine.
