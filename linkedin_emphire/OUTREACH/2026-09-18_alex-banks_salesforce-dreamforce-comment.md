## RECOMMENDED COMMENT (~1053 chars)

Alex — the "layer not replacement" framing is exactly what I keep telling founders who ask me to build "an AI that replaces your CRM."

Nobody rips out Salesforce. They don't even rip out their janky Shopify setup or their six-year-old internal dashboard. They want AI glued onto what they already have.

The hard part isn't building the agent. It's the glue.
→ Mapping actions to undocumented business rules and permissions
→ Making sure the agent can't hallucinate a refund into a system with no undo
→ Deciding what data the agent is allowed to see
→ Handling the one weird edge case only the senior analyst knows about

The labs build agents. The systems of record have the context. The integration engineers writing the boring glue layer are the ones who decide whether any of this actually works in production.

Everyone wants to build layer 3 (the agent). Layers 1, 2, and the glue between them is where the work lives.

You don't replace twenty years of business logic with a prompt. You bolt AI onto it, carefully, and hope it doesn't fall off.
