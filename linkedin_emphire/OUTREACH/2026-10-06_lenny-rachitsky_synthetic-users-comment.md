## COMMENT (paste this)
The thing that decides whether these work is where the simulated user comes from.

If it is a persona you described in a prompt, you are testing what you assumed, and every result confirms your assumptions back at you. That is the confirmation trap wearing a new coat.

If it is built by capturing how real users actually behaved, the messy sessions, the abandoned flows, the inputs nobody expected, then it is a real instrument. And running the same simulation across two model versions usually diverges more than most teams expect, which is worth knowing before you trust a copy decision to it.

Tested anything yourself yet? I am curious whether the output holds up when you check it against real behavior.
