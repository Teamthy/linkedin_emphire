## COMMENT (paste this)
He learned it because he already knew what right looked like.

That's the part the analogy hides. Keying with a CNN works until the edges get hard: fine hair, motion blur, a semi-transparent veil. The model does not tell you it is unsure. It returns a matte that looks plausible, and the artist catches it because they have looked at ten thousand frames and something feels off.

So the sequence is craft first, tool second. The person who knows the workflow and learns the AI can see when the output is wrong. Someone who learns the tool without the craft just gets faster at being wrong.

That is also why these systems get safer when the person running them knows the workflow. They are the first detector.
