## COMMENT (paste this)
Karri's question is answerable. The problem is almost nobody built the instrument.

Shipping speed is easy to measure. Quality is expensive to measure. So teams drift toward the number they can see and read it as progress.

I test AI features for a living, and what I see is not worse products exactly. It is the same mistakes produced much faster. A confident commitment nobody authorized. A permission that was never written down. A number on a slide with no source. All of that existed before. Now it can be generated in an afternoon instead of a sprint.

The teams I would bet on are not the ones running the most agents. They are the ones who kept a small set of known-good examples and re-run them every time the model or the prompt changes. Boring, cheap, and almost nobody does it.
