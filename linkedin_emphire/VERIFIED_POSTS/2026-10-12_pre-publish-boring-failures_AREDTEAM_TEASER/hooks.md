# Hooks: pre-publish boring-failures teaser

Single drafted hook, no A/B set (this was a scheduled pre-show, not a hook-tested post).

## Shipped hook
"Real talk: the scary AI failures in production are boring."

## Rationale
- Reuses the exact phrase that becomes the Article 2 title spine, so the teaser and the article anchor on the same line.
- "Boring" is the tension word: it contradicts the movie-hack expectation the next line sets up.

## Alternate hooks that were considered and not used
- "Last week I tried to break my own chatbot. 4 out of 6 attempts worked." (story-first; saved for the Article 2 opening instead)
- "Your AI won't break in a movie way. It will break on a Tuesday." (kept as a possible repost caption)
