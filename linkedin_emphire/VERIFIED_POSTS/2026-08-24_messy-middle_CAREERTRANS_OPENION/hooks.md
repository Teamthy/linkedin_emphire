# HOOKS — Post #3 (The Messy Middle / Career Transition)

## 5 Hook Options

### Hook A — Quiet truth (RECOMMENDED)
> "Here's the part of the career-switch story nobody posts about: the 6 months where you're not a beginner anymore, but you're definitely not an engineer yet either."
- Type: Hidden truth / universal experience
- Predicted emotion: Recognition + relief ("someone said it")
- Why recommended: Names the specific feeling every switcher recognizes, stops scroll instantly because it's honest (not motivational fluff), sets up the messy-middle arc perfectly.

### Hook B — Bold claim
> "If you're switching into tech and still waiting for the day you feel 'ready,' I've got bad news and good news."
- Type: Teaser / promise of revelation
- Predicted emotion: Curiosity

### Hook C — Admission
> "I see career switchers making the same mistake I made. And it's not about which language they learn."
- Type: Personal mistake → lesson
- Predicted emotion: Self-recognition

### Hook D — Universal question
> "When did you stop feeling like an imposter in your new career? Trick question — you don't."
- Type: Subversive question
- Predicted emotion: Nodding / "wait really?"

### Hook E — Contrarian
> "Tutorials won't get you the job. Your old career won't hold you back. And nobody is going to give you permission to call yourself by the new title."
- Type: Three-part truth bomb
- Predicted emotion: Conviction / motivation

---

**Selected:** Hook A
**Reason:** Best stop-scroll for a Monday workday audience — it's not a hot take, not braggy, not preachy. It's an honest observation that makes everyone mid-switch feel seen before the post even starts. Sets the empathetic tone of the whole piece.
