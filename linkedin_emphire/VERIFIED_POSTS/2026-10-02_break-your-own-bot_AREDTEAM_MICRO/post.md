Quick one for the engineers shipping AI right now.

If you're waiting for someone else to red team your system — a security team, a compliance check, a model provider update — you're already late.

I spent last night trying to break my own support chatbot. Six injection attempts. Four of them worked. One leaked internal system prompts. One made it promise a refund it had no authority to give.

Nobody has to tell you your house has holes when you're the one who finds them.

You don't need a red team certification. You don't need fancy tooling. You need a Tuesday night, a list of ten obvious attacks, and the willingness to look at your own work like someone who wants it to fail.

Start there.
