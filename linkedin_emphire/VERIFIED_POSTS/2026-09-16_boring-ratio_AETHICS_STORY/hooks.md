# Hook Options — 2026-09-16 "boring ratio" post

## HOOK 1 (USED IN DRAFT) — Story hook
"Let me tell you the part of AI they don't show in the keynotes."
- Type: Confession/story
- Emotion: Curiosity ("what part?")
- Strength: Sets up personal story from line 1

## HOOK 2 — Bold claim hook
"Nobody building AI will tell you this: 90% of the job isn't models. It's boring."
- Type: Bold claim
- Emotion: Surprise / "wait really?"
- Strength: Direct contrarian against "AI is magic" narrative

## HOOK 3 — Pattern observation hook (meta from yesterday's threads)
"Funny thing happened yesterday. I spent all day reading LinkedIn threads about AI. Every thread, every take, had the same blind spot."
- Type: Observation
- Emotion: Pattern curiosity
- Strength: Meta, references your own feed behavior — feels authentic

## HOOK 4 — Number hook
"Two hours to build the demo. Twenty-two hours to make sure it doesn't blow up in production."
- Type: Stat/number hook
- Emotion: Specificity, surprise at the ratio
- Strength: Instant credibility via ratio; people stop scrolling for concrete numbers
