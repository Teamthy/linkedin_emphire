One week of testing my own AI features. Biggest surprise:

The attacks that worked were boring.

I expected clever payloads. What got through: a frustrated user typing one word. Or 500 words of nothing pasted into a box.

You don't hack a model. You confuse it. And confusing it takes less patience than writing a bug report.

Every AI feature I built before this week looked fine. Doors look fine until someone tries the handle.
