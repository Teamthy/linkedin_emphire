PART 1: THE OFFER (what Upwork sells, in one line)
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════
Identity: full-stack software engineer (Go, Node.js, TypeScript,
Next.js) who specializes in AI red teaming. Same identity
structure as LinkedIn ("Software Engineer | AI Red Teaming... Web
· Computer Vision · AI Integration") — engineer first, specialist
second, not a dedicated security freelancer.

Productized offer: AI red teaming / LLM & RAG security testing
for teams shipping AI features, sold as 3 fixed-price catalog
tiers (see project_catalog.md) instead of a custom-quote
conversation. The engineering identity is what makes the offer
credible ("I build this stuff, which is how I know where it
breaks"); the catalog is what makes it purchasable.

Proposal targeting still prioritizes AI/chatbot/RAG/agent job
posts first (see proposal_templates.txt) — the dual identity
widens profile discovery and credibility, it doesn't change which
jobs you chase by default. Generic full-stack-only gigs stay
opportunistic, per STRATEGY/2026-10-02_90-day-10k-client-positioning.md.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════
PART 2: PRICING LADDER + REVIEW-FIRST PHILOSOPHY
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════
Upwork reviews cannot be imported from LinkedIn client work
(Sudhir, Twinning, Saiful are LinkedIn-side relationships).
This account starts at zero. The first 3-5 jobs are priced to
WIN REVIEWS, not revenue.

| Phase | Hourly rate | Tier 1 | Tier 2 | Tier 3 | Goal |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Weeks 1-3 (launch) | $65/hr | $150 | $600 | $1,500 | 3-5 reviews, 100% JSS |
| After 5th review | $95-150/hr | $200-250 | $800-1,000 | $2,000+ | Scale toward LinkedIn-side pricing |

Never go below $65/hr to win a job. A cheap first job still
only earns one review; price it right and let the review do
the work, not the discount.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════
PART 4: SIBLING CONSISTENCY WITH LINKEDIN
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════
A buyer who finds both profiles should pass a brand
consistency check:
- Same headshot (real photo, not illustration — matches
  STRATEGY/2026-10-02_90-day-10k-client-positioning.md item 6)
- Same name, same thesis line ("break it before users do")
- Same palette on portfolio/catalog images (navy/cyan system
  from VISUAL/brand_guidelines.txt)
- Same article link (Breaking AI Article 1) referenced in both
  bios

INTENTIONAL DIFFERENCES (do not "fix" these):
- LinkedIn tells the story. Upwork answers the search box.
- LinkedIn CTA is relational ("DM me, tell me what you're
  building"). Upwork CTA is transactional ("Quick Scan,
  $150, 3 days").
- NEVER cross-link with contact info between platforms. An
  article link in the Upwork portfolio is fine. "DM me on
  LinkedIn" anywhere in Upwork copy is a TOS violation —
  see banned_words.txt Category 4.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════
PART 5: WHAT NOT TO DO
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════
- Do not open a second Upwork account to escape the Shopify/
  TikTok history. Duplicate accounts = permanent ban. Pivot
  this one; the e-commerce history is proof of platform trust.
- Do not take generic "build me an AI app" or full-stack dev
  gigs just because they're available. They pay worse, dilute
  the specialty, and work against the search ranking you're
  building for "AI red teaming."
- Do not discount below the pricing ladder to win a job faster.
- Do not let the profile sit half-updated (new title, old
  Shopify overview) — see profile_copy_READY.md section 8.
