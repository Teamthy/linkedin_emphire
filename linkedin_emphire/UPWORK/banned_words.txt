═══════════════════════════════════════════════════
WORDS I NEVER USE IN UPWORK PROFILE COPY OR PROPOSALS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════
Different list from ../banned_words.txt (the LinkedIn one).
LinkedIn banned words are about sounding corporate/AI-generated.
This list is about sounding like every other freelancer a
buyer has already scrolled past. Same root rule either way:
if it could have been copy-pasted by 10,000 other profiles,
cut it.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════
CATEGORY 1: GENERIC FREELANCER OPENERS (instant skip signal)
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════

❌ "Hi there! I'm a hard worker and fast learner"
❌ "I am a dedicated and detail-oriented professional"
❌ "Hello, I hope this finds you well"
❌ "Dear Sir/Madam" / "Dear Hiring Manager"
   Why banned: Upwork clients skim 20+ proposals. Generic
   openers get skipped in under 3 seconds.
   ✅ Use instead: open with their actual product or problem,
      first sentence, no greeting at all.

❌ "I have read your job description and I am a perfect fit"
❌ "I can do this job perfectly"
❌ "I am confident I can exceed your expectations"
   Why banned: Unverifiable claims before any proof is shown.
   ✅ Use instead: state the specific thing you'd test/build
      and the result class (e.g. "6 attempts, 4 breaks").

❌ "Hire me and you won't regret it"
❌ "I guarantee 100% satisfaction"
❌ "Available 24/7"
   Why banned: Sounds desperate, overpromises, invites
   disputes when "guarantee" isn't literal.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════
CATEGORY 2: VAGUE SKILL CLAIMS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════

❌ "Expert in AI" / "AI expert" / "AI guru" / "AI wizard"
   Why banned: Meaningless on a platform flooded with these
   claims since 2023. Signals zero specificity.
   ✅ Use instead: the exact test type — "prompt injection
      testing," "RAG poisoning testing," "LLM red teaming"

❌ "Results-driven" / "Detail-oriented" / "Team player"
❌ "Passionate about technology"
❌ "Jack of all trades"
   Why banned: Resume filler. Says nothing a buyer can
   search for or verify.

❌ "Cutting-edge AI solutions"
❌ "Leveraging the latest AI technology"
   Why banned: Same corporate-AI-speak banned on LinkedIn
   (see ../banned_words.txt Category 1). Doubly bad here
   because it reads like it was written by the AI being
   tested, not the person testing it.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════
CATEGORY 3: PRICE/VALUE DESPERATION SIGNALS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════

❌ "Will work for cheap" / "Negotiable, name your price"
❌ "I need this job badly"
❌ "New to Upwork but very skilled" (as a leading line)
   Why banned: Anchors the buyer on price or pity instead
   of the work. Zero reviews is a fact, not an apology.
   ✅ Use instead: let the catalog price anchor the
      conversation. If asked about being new, answer plainly:
      "This account is new to AI security specifically — the
      e-commerce work above is the platform history."

❌ "Final price" overused in every message
❌ Excessive emoji in proposals (🔥💯🚀 stacked)
   Why banned: Reads as low-effort mass-send, not a tailored
   pitch to this specific job post.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════
CATEGORY 4: TOS-RISK PHRASES (never write these, period)
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════

❌ "Contact me on LinkedIn / WhatsApp / email for the real rate"
❌ "Let's take this off Upwork to save the fee"
❌ "Pay me directly and I'll give you a discount"
   Why banned: Violates Upwork ToS (off-platform payment
   circumvention). Can get the account suspended or banned.
   This is not a style preference, it's a hard account-safety
   rule — see UPWORK/README.md hard rule #2.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════
CATEGORY 5: OVER-HEDGING (undermines credibility)
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════

❌ "I'm not really a security person, but..."
❌ "I'm still learning this, so..."
❌ "Just a beginner at red teaming"
   Why banned: Same rule as the LinkedIn voice DNA — state
   what you did and what it found. Let the work speak. Don't
   pre-apologize for a skill you're charging for.
   ✅ Use instead: "You don't need to be a security
      specialist to need this tested" (buyer-facing framing,
      not self-deprecating).

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════
WHAT TO SAY INSTEAD — THE HOUSE STYLE
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════
Open with their problem. State the exact test type. Give one
concrete proof point (the 6-attempts-4-breaks story, or a
specific finding). State the deliverable in plain terms (a
findings report with repro steps and fixes). Price it. Stop.
No flattery, no filler, no apology.
