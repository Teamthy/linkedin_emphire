This folder is the Upwork counterpart to the LinkedIn empire. Same man, same
identity (**software engineer, full-stack developer, who specializes in AI
red teaming**), same thesis ("I build AI features, and I'm the one who
breaks them before your users do"), same brand palette and headshot —
different *function*. Read `master_prompt.txt` first if you're dropping this
into an AI tool to get help running it.

**LinkedIn:** www.linkedin.com/in/olusanya-timothy — the stage. Tells the
story, builds authority, earns inbound DMs and referral-quality leads.

**Upwork:** upwork.com/freelancers/~01a65712b8b0954fd2 — the counter.
Search-matched, package-first, review-fed. Buyers who are already typing
"AI red teaming," "prompt injection testing," or "full-stack developer"
into a search box and want to hire *today*, not follow a thought leader.


## Why both exist
LinkedIn leads are warm but slow (90-day arc to the first $10k, per
`STRATEGY/2026-10-02_90-day-10k-client-positioning.md`). Upwork leads are
colder but faster to convert into a paid first job once the profile ranks,
and Upwork reviews become proof-of-work LinkedIn can't fake. Running both
means the LinkedIn content funnel and the Upwork search funnel compound each
other without duplicating effort — one thesis, two acquisition channels.

## Hard rules (do not violate — see `PLANS/2026-10-10_upwork-rebrand-empire.md` for full detail)
1. **Never open a second account.** Pivot this one. Duplicate accounts = permanent ban, and the Shopify/TikTok history is proof of platform trust, not a liability to hide.
2. **No contact info, no "find me on LinkedIn," no off-platform payment — ever, anywhere on Upwork.** First clients stay inside escrow. The 10% fee buys buyer protection, and it protects you too.
3. **Reviews don't transfer.** Upwork starts at zero. Price the first 3-5 jobs to win 100% JSS and reviews, not to win money. Raise the rate after review 5, not before.
