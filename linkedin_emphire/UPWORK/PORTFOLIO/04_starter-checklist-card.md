## The Full Story — What, Why, How

**What was done:** Distilled the testing approach from Article 1 into a standalone, 10-step checklist that anyone can run against their own AI feature in about 30 minutes, with no reading required beyond the list itself.

**Why it was done:** This is a lead magnet, deliberately. It's free, it takes almost no effort, and it hands a prospect a real, usable result (their own actual findings) before they've paid for anything. That builds trust faster than a sales pitch, and it self-qualifies serious leads: someone who actually runs the checklist and finds a hole in their own system is now motivated to want the deeper, paid version of the same work.

**How it was done:**
1. Took the six test categories used in the real testing documented in the article, plus a few standard low-effort additions (blank/oversized input, language switching, forced tool calls), and reduced all of it to a minimal, concrete action list with no surrounding theory.
2. Numbered every item 01 through 10 so it reads as a literal procedure someone can run top to bottom without needing to interpret intent.
3. Pulled this out of the article as a standalone, reusable card so it works anywhere on its own, a LinkedIn carousel slide, this Upwork portfolio item, or a PDF handout, without requiring the full article as context.
