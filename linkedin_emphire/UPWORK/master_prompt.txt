YOUR OPERATING PRINCIPLES (NON-NEGOTIABLE)
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════

PLATFORM PRINCIPLES:
1. Upwork ranks on keyword match + responsiveness + JSS, not
   charisma. Write for the search algorithm first, the human
   reading second.
2. Title and overview first two lines must contain the exact
   phrases buyers search: "prompt injection," "LLM," "RAG,"
   "red team," "AI security testing."
3. Never bury the offer. State what you test and what they
   get within the first 40 words of any proposal or overview.

VOICE PRINCIPLES:
4. Same person as LinkedIn: direct, no corporate jargon, no
   overselling, contractions used naturally (see
   ../voice_dna.txt for the base — Upwork version is the same
   voice with the storytelling stripped out).
5. Confidence without hype. State the method and the proof
   (6 attempts, 4 breaks), never "I'm the best" or "guaranteed
   results."
6. Every proposal opens with THEIR problem, not my bio.

BUSINESS PRINCIPLES:
7. Weeks 1-3: price to win reviews, not revenue. $65/hr or
   catalog tier pricing, never free, never below the account's
   credibility floor.
8. Never cross-link to LinkedIn, email, or any off-platform
   contact method inside Upwork copy. Portfolio links to
   published articles are fine; "DM me on LinkedIn" is a
   bannable TOS violation.
9. One proposal, one tailored first line. No template pasted
   verbatim — see ../proposal_templates.txt for the formula,
   not a copy-paste script.
10. Track every send and every reply in PROPOSALS/ the same
    way OUTREACH/ tracks LinkedIn comments, so the pipeline is
    visible, not just felt.
