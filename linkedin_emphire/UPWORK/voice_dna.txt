UPWORK VOICE — same person, compressed for search + buyers
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════
Base voice is ../voice_dna.txt (the full LinkedIn voice DNA).
Everything there about tone, formality (6/10), contractions,
and "never corporate, never fake" still applies. This file
only covers what's DIFFERENT about writing for Upwork.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════
WHAT CARRIES OVER UNCHANGED
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════
→ Formality level 6/10 — professional enough for a CTO reading
  proposals, human enough to not sound like a template
→ Contractions always: I'm, don't, can't, it's, here's
→ Honest admissions over fake authority ("I built that thing
  and still found four ways in" beats "I'm an AI security
  expert")
→ Concrete numbers over adjectives (6 attempts, 4 breaks —
  not "thorough testing")
→ Zero corporate jargon (../banned_words.txt still applies on
  top of ./banned_words.txt)

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════
WHAT'S DIFFERENT HERE
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════

1. NO STORYTELLING ARC.
   LinkedIn posts build to an insight over 800-1500 characters.
   Upwork buyers skim. Lead with the service and the proof in
   the first two sentences. Save story for the "why me" portion
   only, after the offer is already clear.

2. SEARCH KEYWORDS ARE NOT OPTIONAL STYLE CHOICES.
   On LinkedIn, repeating "prompt injection" three times would
   read stiff. On Upwork, the title and first lines of the
   overview need the literal phrases buyers search: "prompt
   injection," "LLM," "RAG," "AI red teaming," "jailbreak
   testing." Natural voice still applies to the SENTENCES around
   those phrases, but don't paraphrase the keywords away.

3. THE CTA IS TRANSACTIONAL, NOT RELATIONAL.
   LinkedIn CTA: "DM me, tell me what you're building, I reply
   to every note" (relationship-first).
   Upwork CTA: "Check the catalog for the $150 Quick Scan" or
   "Send a proposal with your stack and I'll scope it in a day"
   (action-first — buyers want a next step, not a conversation
   starter).

4. PROOF REPLACES PERSONALITY.
   Upwork overview should spend fewer words on who you are and
   more on what you tested and what it found. The "funny
   enough..." / "honestly..." conversational openers from the
   core voice DNA are too soft for a 6-second skim. Keep them
   for proposal body text (once they're already reading), not
   the headline or opening line of the overview.

5. NO EM-DASH, NO HASHTAGS, NO QUESTION-BAIT CLOSERS.
   LinkedIn posts end on an engagement question ("What's the
   dumbest input that ever broke something you built? 👇").
   Upwork overview/proposals end on a clear next step instead:
   what to click, what to send, or what happens after they hire.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════
QUICK TEST BEFORE PUBLISHING ANY UPWORK COPY
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════
1. Does a buyer searching "prompt injection testing" find the
   exact phrase in the first 2 lines? If no, rewrite.
2. Could this paragraph have been written by any of the 50
   other AI-security profiles on Upwork? If yes, add the
   specific number/detail that only this account has.
3. Is there a clear next action in the last line? If it ends
   on vibes instead of an action, cut the last line and add one
   that isn't.
