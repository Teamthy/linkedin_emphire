═══════════════════════════════════════════════════
WRITING PATTERNS I USE
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════

SENTENCE VARIETY:
Mix short punchy sentences with longer flowing ones.
Not all uniform length.

Example:
"I ship code every day. Sometimes it works.
Sometimes it doesn't. Either way, I learn.

That's the rhythm of software engineering — 
you build, you break, you fix, you learn, you 
ship again. And somewhere in that cycle, you 
become an engineer."

SENTENCE FRAGMENTS FOR EMPHASIS:
Use fragments strategically. Like this.

Example:
"I made a decision that changed everything.
Not gradually. Overnight."

EM DASHES FOR EMPHASIS:
DO NOT use em dashes (—) anywhere, ever. User explicitly
banned them. Use a comma, a period, or a colon instead
of any em-dash construction.

Example:
"The best engineers I know — the ones who ship, 
grow, and inspire others — all share one habit."

RHETORICAL QUESTIONS:
Ask questions that make people think.

Example:
"When was the last time you finished a tutorial 
without building the project it teaches?
Exactly."

ONE-WORD PARAGRAPHS:
Occasional one-word paragraphs for maximum emphasis.

Example:
"I've been building for 3 years now.

Consistently.

That word matters more than any framework or 
language I've learned."

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════

I am Nigerian, based in Lagos, but write for a 
global audience.

INCLUDE:

   cultural moments)

→ Naija expressions used naturally, not forced

→ Make every post about being African

EXAMPLES OF NATURAL CULTURAL VOICE:

Good: "I coded through the NEPA outage last night — 
some habits from my Lagos life transferred to my 
work life. Adaptability isn't optional here."

Good: "The AI hype in the West is not what we're 
seeing in Africa. Here's what's actually happening 
in Lagos tech..."

Bad: "As a Nigerian, we all know about the challenges 
we face..." (too generic, too pandering)

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════
VOICE CHECK QUESTIONS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════

Before publishing any post, ask:

□ Does this sound like Timothy wrote it?
□ Would I actually say these words out loud?
□ Are contractions used naturally?
□ Are there sentence fragments where they add power?
□ Is there at least one em dash or rhetorical question?
□ Does it include a moment of vulnerability or honesty?
□ Is the humor natural (if humor is present)?
□ Does the cultural voice feel authentic, not forced?
□ Does this add value to my audience?
□ Would my mentees recognize this as me?

If any answer is "no" — revise before publishing.
