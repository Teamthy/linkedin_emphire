## 2. THE ENGINES WE TAKE FROM THE BOOKS

Each engine lists the book, what it says in short form, and exactly where it goes in EMPIRE.

### Engine 1 — The Value Equation (Hormozi, *$100M Offers*)
**Core idea:** Perceived value ≈ (dream outcome × belief it will work) ÷ (time to result × effort and hassle). Raise the top two, shrink the bottom two.

**Apply to our offers:**
- **Dream outcome** is what the buyer wants, not what we deliver. "Find out if your bot leaks before your users do" beats "15-point injection scan." Rewrite titles and first lines to lead with the outcome.
- **Likelihood** comes from receipts: 6 attempts, 4 breaks, the Breaking AI series, the sample findings PDF. Put these in the first screen of every listing and proposal.
- **Time delay**: Quick Scan is 3 days. Say so in the first line. Shorter time to a first result is the cheapest value we can add.
- **Effort**: the buyer sends access and answers the intake. We run everything. Say "you do nothing but send access" explicitly.

**Where it lives:** `UPWORK/project_catalog.md` (titles, descriptions), proposal opener, every DM.

### Engine 2 — Grand Slam Offer (Hormozi, *$100M Offers*)
**Core idea:** Build an offer so specific and stacked that comparison shopping stops working. The method goes: pick a starving crowd → list every problem between them and the outcome → solve those problems inside the offer → trim and stack → add enhancers (scarcity, urgency, bonuses, guarantee, naming).

**Apply:**
- **Starving crowd:** teams that shipped an LLM feature in the last 6 months and have no security loop. Health, finance, and regulated-workflow teams come first.
- **Problem list per tier:** injection, system-prompt leaks, RAG poisoning, forged tool calls, runaway cost. Each becomes a line item in the Standard and Advanced tiers.
- **Enhancers we can actually stand behind:**
  - *Naming:* "Pre-Launch Hardening," "Red Team Review" (already done)
  - *Bonus:* a free re-test of fixes within 14 days on Standard (needs owner sign-off on the time cost)
  - *Guarantee:* a re-test that confirms each fix holds, or the next round is free (**needs owner approval, do not publish without it**)
  - *Scarcity:* 2 intro slots per month at the lower rate (only if it's true)

**Where it lives:** Standard and Advanced tier descriptions; the 90-day doc's offer section.

### Engine 3 — The Core Four (Hormozi, *$100M Leads*)
**Core idea:** There are four ways to create leads. Warm outreach (people who know you), free content (one-to-many, warm), cold outreach (one-to-one, cold), paid ads (one-to-many, cold). Start with the warm ones because they are cheapest and convert best. Lead getters (customers who refer, partners, affiliates) multiply everything. A lead is someone who took an action (opted in, replied, booked), not someone who saw a post.

**Apply:**

| Core Four method | EMPIRE channel | Cadence |
|---|---|---|
| Warm outreach | Sudhir, Twinning, Saiful, existing LinkedIn 1st-degree network, Upwork clients | 3 personal messages/week |
| Free content | LinkedIn posts + Breaking AI articles + the free 30-minute test | 2 posts/week, 1 article/month |
| Cold outreach | Target list in `OPPURTUNITIES/`, comments on buyer posts (free). Blank connection requests within LinkedIn's free limits | 5/week, comments daily |
| Paid ads | **Deferred.** Only after the first $1k is booked. LinkedIn Ads test budget: $100 max | Week 8+ |

**Lead getters:**
- Every finished Upwork client gets a review request (within 24h, per plan) and a referral ask at delivery, using Hormozi's "ask at the moment of value" idea.
- Sudhir and Saiful are lead-getters for the LinkedIn lane.
- Agencies that ship AI features are the affiliate target for Pre-Launch Hardening.

**Lead magnet (the "free thing" every post points to):**
The free 30-minute test from the latest article. It has to solve one narrow problem fully (test a chatbot against 5 injection patterns) and reveal the next problem (the other 15 patterns, RAG, and agents need a real review). Keep it free, keep it small, and make the paid step obvious at the end.

**Rule of 100, adapted:** The book's version is 100 actions a day, which is too much for one person. Our version is **30 minutes a day** of primary actions (comment, reply, or send), 6 days a week. Track the count. Quantity matters more than talent here.

### Engine 4 — The Money Model (Hormozi, *$100M Money Models*)
**Core idea:** Stack offers in sequence so that a customer pays back the cost of acquiring them inside 30 days. Four offer types: **Attraction** (turns strangers into buyers), **Upsell** (raises the first purchase), **Downsell** (turns a "no" into a smaller "yes"), **Continuity** (recurring revenue). The book's warning is to build one stage at a time, because building the whole thing at once collapses cash flow.

**Apply to Upwork and LinkedIn:**

| Stage | What we sell | Status |
|---|---|---|
| 1 — Attraction | Quick Scan at $200, 3 days. The entry point for every new buyer | **Build now**, perfect this first |
| 2 — Upsell | Standard at $800. Quick Scan credit counts toward it (already in catalog copy) | Already in copy |
| 2 — Downsell | When a buyer says no to Advanced, offer Standard with the Advanced items split off as add-ons | Write in week 4 |
| 3 — Continuity | Monthly re-test retainer after Advanced (regression run + one new attack class per month) | Week 6+, after 2 Advanced jobs |

**Rule for us:** Do not add stage 3 until stage 1 is converting. The plan's order is Attraction → Upsell → Downsell → Continuity.

### Engine 5 — The Larger Market Formula + HVCO (Sabri Suby, *Sell Like Crazy*)
**Core idea:** Most people sell to the 3% who are already buying, and ignore the rest. Suby's breakdown is roughly 3% ready to buy, 17% gathering information, 20% problem-aware but not shopping, 60% not thinking about it. The move is to create a **High-Value Content Offer (HVCO)**: a free asset that solves a real problem and earns the right to the sale. Then keep following up. He calls the follow-up the part that pays.

**Apply:**
- **Target the 37%** (gathering info + problem-aware). They are the teams with an AI feature in review or a recent incident. The 3% are already on Upwork looking at Quick Scan.
- **HVCO = the free 30-minute test + the sample findings report.** Both are free and both prove we can do the work.
- **Hook → Story → Offer** on every post: a bold line about how AI fails, a specific break from the 6-attempt log, and a clear next step (the free test or a Quick Scan).
- **Relentless follow-up:** a 4-touch sequence for every warm lead over 14 days. Touch 1 is the test, touch 2 is a finding, touch 3 is a case note, touch 4 is a direct offer.

**Where it lives:** `STRATEGY/` (targeting), `EMPIRE/content_bank.txt` (hook bank), `OUTREACH/` (follow-up sequences).

### Engine 6 — Speaking as a Lead Engine (cross-book)
Speaking is not in any of the four books in the form we need, so this is our own build. It is the one area the plan cannot replace.

- **Talk ideas (one per month):**
  - "How to break your own AI chatbot before your users do" (Lagos AI / dev meetups)
  - "Prompt injection in 15 minutes: what a Quick Scan actually finds" (online, recorded)
  - "What your red team report should say" (leadership audience, feeds the Advanced tier)
- **The workshop offer** from the 90-day doc ($2,000, 2-hour team workshop) doubles as a speaking follow-on.
- **Every talk ends with the free 30-minute test link.** The talk is the warm-up, the test is the lead capture.
- **Target 3 speaking slots in 70 days:** 1 Lagos in-person meetup, 1 online meetup, 1 podcast or newsletter guest spot.

## 3. THE EMPIRE ENGINE (how the engines connect)

```
  ATTRACT (Core Four: warm, content, cold, ads)
      ↓
  HVCO: free 30-min test / sample findings report   ← Engine 5
      ↓
  OFFER: Value Equation + Grand Slam stack          ← Engines 1-2
      ↓
  MONEY MODEL: Quick Scan → Standard → Advanced     ← Engine 4
      ↓
  DELIVER: four-part findings report, debrief call
      ↓
  REVIEW + REFERRAL (lead getters)                  ← Engine 3
      ↓
  SPEAKING + CONTENT built from the delivered work  ← Engine 6
      ↓
  WEEKLY SCOREBOARD → Sunday planning → loop
```

**The loop is the point.** Every paid job becomes a review, a case note, a talk example, and a post. The sample findings report is already built for this.

## 6. RISKS AND HOW WE HANDLE THEM

| Risk | How we handle it |
|---|---|
| Upwork proposals get no replies | Test the opener weekly. Track reply rate per opener. Replace the worst one. |
| Project 2 has no pricing yet | Decide flat vs company-size before any Project 2 proposals go out. |
| LinkedIn free limits cap cold outreach | Lean on comments and warm outreach. Start Premium trial on schedule. |
| Consistency breaks (it did in Aug–Sep) | Fixed schedule: 2 posts/week + 30 min/day. Sunday planning is non-negotiable. |
| Buyers ask for off-platform payment | Hard rule: stays inside Upwork escrow. No contact info on any Upwork asset. |
| Overpromising guarantees | No guarantee goes public without owner approval and a real delivery plan behind it. |
| Speaking slots never come | Pitch 2/week. Treat the free test as the deliverable even without a stage. |
