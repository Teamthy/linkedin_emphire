PART 1: PIPELINE MODIFICATIONS (from analytics data)
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════

The 90-day data told us 6 concrete things. We now bake them into every post.

## 1. DEFAULT POST STRUCTURE = BOLAJI-SHORT (ESSAYS ARE BANNED)
Data: tutorial-hell essay = 190 impressions / 1 engagement. Bug-not-in-code (short, personal, 3 bullets, PS question) = 683 / 10. AI hoax repost (short, 6 bullets, PS question) = 4,443+ / ~22.

Required structure for every standalone post:
- Hook (1 short line, personal or bold)
- Context (1-2 paragraphs, short, human)
- The point (list of 3-5 short bullets — never more than 5)
- "One rule I ship by" — screenshot/save line (MANDATORY — this fixes the zero-saves problem)
- PS question that invites a war story / experience reply (MANDATORY — drives comments)
- 3 hashtags max
- TARGET LENGTH: 800-1200 chars (the bug post was ~1000; AI hoax was ~900; essay was ~1800+ and flopped)

If you can't fit it in 1200 chars, cut it. The point isn't to say everything — it's to give people one thing they remember.

## 2. MANDATORY SCREENSHOT LINE PER POST
Data: zero reposts, zero saves across the old posts. Saves/reposts happen when there's one sentence people want to keep or send to a coworker.

Every post must have ONE sentence that stands alone as quotable. Examples you already have:
- "Production is a neighborhood, not a house." (bug post)
- "The demo is not the product." (friday-cutoff)
- "AI didn't make engineering easier, it made bad engineering faster." (Tesla thread)
- "People talk about code quality like it's a skill problem. A lot of the time it's a calendar problem." (friday-cutoff)

No post ships without this line.

## 3. POSTING SCHEDULE (DOABLE, NOT PUNITIVE)
Data: 3 posts in 90 days = zero momentum. 5 posts/week burns you out.

Fixed cadence (in WAT):
- WED 12:00 PM — Software Engineering pillar (technical, builder, production stories)
- FRI 12:00 PM — AI Ethics / Career Builder pillar (alternating weeks)
- SUN 10:00 AM (optional) — short personal / faith / reflection post, no pressure

That's 2 guaranteed posts/week = ~8-9 posts in 30 days, enough to rebuild algorithm momentum without fatigue.

## 4. ENGAGEMENT WINDOW = 90 MINUTES AFTER POSTING
Data: the AI hoax post hit 4.4K impressions because you were online replying in the first 90 minutes.

Hard rule: for 90 minutes after every post, you are online.
- Reply to every serious comment (like + real reply)
- Like funny/short comments
- Don't argue with trolls
- This 90-minute window is when the algorithm decides whether to push your post further. Skipping it kills 60-70% of potential reach.

## 5. PILLAR ROTATION (from data, not theory)
Data: your SE identity is primary. AI is the specialization your audience is most curious about. Career builder pulls entry-level engineers.

Per 4-week cycle:
- 2 SE / technical craft posts
- 2 AI Ethics / responsible AI posts
- 2 Career Builder / learning posts
- 1 Personal / faith (Sunday, optional)
- 0 pure hype / "AI will change everything" posts (banned words list covers this)

## 6. PROOF-OF-WORK POSTS EVERY 3RD POST
Every third post should include a specific thing you actually built or shipped — not just opinion. Example: sorghum CV work, a production bug, an AI integration you built, a red teaming finding from going through Tarique's guide. These are the posts recruiters screenshot.
