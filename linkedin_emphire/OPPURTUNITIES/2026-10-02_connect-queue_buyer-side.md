## READ THIS FIRST (honesty block)

Three things before the names:

1. **Mega accounts will not convert.** Anyone above ~50K followers does not accept cold requests and does not buy $3,500 engagements. Simon Willison, Endrit, Satya, Allie at 1.65M: those are follow-and-comment accounts. Comment reach, not connection requests.

2. **The money comes from three places, in this order:**
   - Operators at companies that ship AI and have 50 to 500 people. Real budget, real risk, nobody owning it.
   - Consultants and agency owners who subcontract. They already have clients with AI in production.
   - Lagos and Africa network. Local trust, no timezone gap, no "why is this person overseas" tax.

3. **The real conversion math.** Of every 10 specific notes sent: 3 to 5 accept, 1 to 2 turn into a real conversation, and roughly 1 in 30 becomes a paid engagement within 90 days. That is normal. It is also why we send 5 a week for 12 weeks, not 50 in one night. LinkedIn flags spam above ~5/day and the cap in the playbook is 5/week.

**Rules that apply to every name below:**
- Note is ≤200 characters, references something specific they built or wrote, ends with "- T", asks for nothing.
- If marked "engage first": comment on 1 to 2 of their posts before sending. If marked "send directly," mid-size operators do not require warming up.
- After they accept: no pitch for 3 to 7 days. Comment on their next post first. Then a short DM. The pitch comes only after they ask what you are working on, or after two exchanges.
- Never more than 5 connection requests in a week. Order matters, so use the queue at the bottom.
