## THE RULE

When a bell fires: **comment within 15 minutes or skip it.** Early comments sit at the top and get read by everyone who opens the post later.

- 1 comment, 2 to 4 sentences. Add a detail the post didn't say, from a builder's seat.
- No links. No pitching. No "DM me." Ask nothing.
- Cap it at 2 to 4 of these per day. This is a sniper rifle, not a firehose.

**Best windows (WAT):**
- 8:30 to 10:00 AM → UK, EU, Lagos mornings
- 2:00 to 4:00 PM → US East mornings (9 to 11 AM ET)
