CATEGORY 1: CORPORATE/AI SPEAK
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════

❌ "Delve into" → "explore" or "look at"
❌ "Landscape" → "space" or "field"
❌ "Leverage" → "use" or "apply"
❌ "Unlock" → "open up" or "access"
❌ "Game-changer" → describe specific outcome
❌ "Revolutionary" → describe specifically
❌ "Cutting-edge" → "new" or "recent"
❌ "Elevate" → "improve" or "raise"
❌ "Streamline" → "simplify"
❌ "Robust" → "reliable" or "strong"
❌ "Harness" → "use"
❌ "Navigate" → "work through"
❌ "Tapestry" → nothing (word is dead)
❌ "Comprehensive" → "complete" or "full"
❌ "Furthermore" → "also" or "and"
❌ "In conclusion" → just conclude naturally
❌ "It's worth noting" → just say the thing
❌ "Pivotal" → "important"
❌ "In today's world" → start with content
❌ "At the end of the day" → "ultimately"
❌ "Moving forward" → "going forward" or "next"
❌ "Synergy" → never appropriate
❌ "Holistic approach" → "complete approach"
❌ "Best practices" → "what works"
❌ "Value proposition" → "value" or "benefit"
❌ "Circle back" → "come back to"
❌ "Deep dive" → "look closer"
❌ "Actionable insights" → "practical lessons"
❌ "Key takeaways" → "main lessons"
❌ "Touch base" → "connect"

CATEGORY 5: WEAK WRITING PATTERNS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════

❌ Starting with "As a [X]..."
❌ "Just wanted to share..."
❌ "Hot take:" (overused)
❌ "Little did I know..."
❌ "Life-changing..."
❌ "Absolutely!" / "Totally!"
❌ "Amazing!" / "Awesome!"

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════
CATEGORY 6: PHRASES THAT SOUND SMART BUT AREN'T
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════

❌ "It goes without saying" → just make the point
❌ "That being said" → "However" or "But"
❌ "At this point in time" → "Now"
❌ "In order to" → "To"
❌ "The fact of the matter is" → state the fact
❌ "For all intents and purposes" → "Basically"
❌ "At the same time" → "Meanwhile"

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════
THE ULTIMATE TEST
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════

Before publishing, read aloud:

"Would I actually say these words to a friend
at a coffee shop?"

If NO → Rewrite in natural voice.
If YES → Publish with confidence.

Real people say: "Let's use what we have to build
something new."

Real people don't say: "Let's leverage synergies to
unlock cutting-edge value propositions."

Write like you talk. Just cleaner.
