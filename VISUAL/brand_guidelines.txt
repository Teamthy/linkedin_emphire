╔═══════════════════════════════════════════════╗
║ SECTION 2: VISUAL IDENTITY                     ║
╚═══════════════════════════════════════════════╝

COLOR PALETTE:

PRIMARY COLORS:
- Deep Navy: #0a1428 (backgrounds)
- White: #FFFFFF (primary text)

SECONDARY COLORS:
- Soft Cyan: #8BB8E8 (accent text)
- Medium Navy: #1a2a4f (secondary backgrounds)

NEVER USE:
- Neon colors
- Multiple bright accents
- Gradients on text
- Rainbow effects

TYPOGRAPHY:

EDITORIAL HEADLINES:
- Font: Playfair Display (serif)
- Weight: Regular
- Use: Featured images, major headlines
- Style: NO italic unless intentional

BODY TEXT / SUB-HEADLINES:
- Font: Inter
- Weight: Regular
- Use: Sub-headlines, body copy

ACCENT TEXT:
- Font: Inter
- Weight: Medium or Regular
- Size: Small (14-18pt)
- Use: Taglines, labels

CODE TEXT (If applicable):
- Font: JetBrains Mono
- Use: Code snippets in carousels

DESIGN PRINCIPLES:
1. RESTRAINT: Remove elements, don't add them
2. WHITE SPACE: Generous margins always
3. NATURAL PHOTOS: No fake processing or glow
4. NO DECORATIONS: No dots, circuits, generic tech
5. EDITORIAL FEEL: Magazine quality, not template
6. CONFIDENCE: Let the message speak

PHOTO GUIDELINES:
- Natural skin tones (no color casts)
- No glow effects around edges
- Clean background removal
- Professional attire
- Direct or 3/4 camera angle
- Warm, approachable expression
- No heavy filters

DESIGN DON'TS:
✗ No logo marks (OT initials, etc.)
✗ No generic tech decorations
✗ No AI-looking elements
✗ No underline accents under text
✗ No multiple text effects
✗ No stock imagery
✗ No corporate template feel

║ SECTION 3: VERBAL IDENTITY                     ║
╚═══════════════════════════════════════════════╝

TONE OF VOICE:
- Professional but warm
- Direct but not harsh
- Bold when needed
- Conversational always
- Thoughtful
- Anti-hype

HOW WE SOUND:
✓ "Honestly, this is what I've found..."
✓ "Here's the thing about..."
✓ "In my experience..."
✗ NOT "In today's rapidly evolving landscape..."
✗ NOT "We must leverage cutting-edge solutions..."

CONTENT POSITIONING:
- Software Engineer FIRST
- AI/CV as specializations
- Multi-domain builder
- Teacher and mentor
- Nigerian identity as asset
- Anti-hype always

MESSAGING HIERARCHY:
1. "I build real software" (primary)
2. "Across multiple domains" (differentiator)
3. "Including AI and Computer Vision" (specialization)
4. "And teach others to do the same" (mission)
5. "From Lagos, Nigeria" (identity)

╔═══════════════════════════════════════════════╗
║ SECTION 4: CONTENT BRAND RULES                 ║
╚═══════════════════════════════════════════════╝

ALWAYS DO:
✓ Lead with Software Engineering identity
✓ Include personal experience
✓ Use contractions naturally
✓ Show multi-domain range
✓ Reference Nigerian context when natural
✓ Balance technical + personal + wisdom
✓ End posts with genuine questions
✓ Maintain anti-hype positioning

NEVER DO:
✗ Lead with AI as primary identity
✗ Use banned words (see banned_words.txt)
✗ Sound corporate or generic
✗ Post without value
✗ Chase trends over depth
✗ Fake expertise you don't have
✗ Be political or divisive
✗ Sell in every post

╔═══════════════════════════════════════════════╗
║ SECTION 5: PLATFORM-SPECIFIC GUIDELINES        ║
╚═══════════════════════════════════════════════╝

LINKEDIN:
- Professional but warm
- 800-1500 characters
- Formatted with line breaks
- Bold key phrases
- 3 hashtags maximum
- Natural CTA at end

X (TWITTER):
- More casual
- Under 280 chars per tweet
- Punchier language
- Hot takes okay
- Threads for depth

SUBSTACK:
- Most personal
- 1500-2500 words
- Full depth exploration
- Vulnerable when appropriate
- Reader relationship focused
