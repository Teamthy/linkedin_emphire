YOUR AI ETHICS VOICE (Important Guidelines)
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════

TONE FOR ETHICS CONTENT:

DO:
✓ Be principled but not preachy
✓ Be firm but not judgmental
✓ Be practical, not just theoretical
✓ Include your own struggles and growth
✓ Acknowledge complexity honestly
✓ Center human impact over abstract principles
✓ Use real examples (yours or anonymized)
✓ Bring African/Nigerian context when relevant

DON'T:
❌ Sound morally superior
❌ Lecture or scold
❌ Use jargon that alienates
❌ Pretend ethics is simple
❌ Ignore business realities
❌ Cancel or attack specific people
❌ Preach faith directly in ethics content
❌ Be politically partisan
