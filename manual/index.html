The check is the product — one-page primer

Break → Measure → Publish the check. Most people can break a demo. Few can turn a break into a reproducible number anyone can rerun and question. You do both. The honest number, not the clean one: “2/5 (40%), t=0, n=5, one provision, one model” — with its limitation. I build the check, not the claim. I would not report a number I could not reproduce.

Chapter 0 — Orientation: Why Red Team, and Who You’re Building to Be

F The Idea The failure is found faster by a motivated stranger. Red team = find, measure, make the fix reproducible — not “prove it’s bad.” Thesis: The check is the product.

F Two hats: Breaker (finds the failure) + Researcher (makes it a number/method/published check). Owning both is rare. Ethics: consent + scope = red team; without either, it’s an attack.

Worked Examples — 0.1 & 0.2

0.1 Workplace ranking (Art. 5(1)(f)): 12 models, 3000+ runs, compliance 7–54%, Art. 5 violated ~80% — same scenario anchors Lab 5.

0.2 System-prompt leak: screenshot ≠ finding. Harness 3/20 (15%) with fingerprint = defensible.

Lab 0 — State the thesis out loud

Target: yourself. Write thesis + two hats + one line + safe-practice boundary (5 bullets). Deliverable: 1 page you can recite in 60 s.

Pitfall Opinion without a rerun; screenshot without a count. Missing check: harness that prints pass/fail with a denominator.

Key terms: red team, researcher, the check, reproducibility, responsible disclosure. Ready-when: State thesis + two hats + one line in one breath + hand someone your boundary.

Chapter 5 — Evals, Measurement & the Number ★ The Core (longest)

F The Idea Task + input + judge + metric + crisp pass — definition of pass is the whole eval. Number with denominator; honest vs clean number; shared ruler.

P The Mechanics Inspect: Task/Sample/dataset/solver/scorer/metrics/model_roles/GenerateConfig/eval_config. CLI inspect eval / inspect view. LLM-as-judge: 1 vs board of 3 + majority reducer; biases (position/verbosity/self-preferential/sycophancy). LARA: 10 scenarios, verbatim law, 3 judges, 3000+ runs, 96.7% agreement, 50+ hrs review.

Worked Examples 5.1–5.3

5.1 1 judge 2/5 (40%) vs board 3/5 (60%) — one flip from verbosity bias. 5.2 Same model t=0 4/10 (40%) vs t=0.8 1/10 (10%). 5.3 Two-sided boundary — over-escalate (false alarms) vs under-escalate (confident-wrong ships).

Lab 5 — LARA-lite (7 steps, the most valuable lab in the book)
