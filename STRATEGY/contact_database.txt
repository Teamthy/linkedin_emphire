╔═══════════════════════════════════════════════╗
║ CRM MANAGEMENT RULES                           ║
╚═══════════════════════════════════════════════╝

WEEKLY (Every Friday — 15 min):
□ Add new contacts from the week
□ Update last interaction dates
□ Set next actions for priority contacts
□ Review anyone needing follow-up

MONTHLY (1st of month — 30 min):
□ Audit relationship strength levels
□ Move people between tiers
□ Identify dormant relationships to revive
□ Plan revival messages for 5-10 contacts

QUARTERLY (Every 90 days):
□ Full relationship audit
□ Remove truly dead contacts
□ Upgrade promising relationships
□ Strategic connection planning
□ Opportunity pipeline review
